# Market validation gates

PrintableCheckList will validate the family-trip final-packing position through a narrow self-service Beta rather than a pre-development concierge or in-person experiment.

## Beta price

Use a US base price of $2.99 for the one-time Pro purchase and let App Store Connect generate equivalent prices for other storefronts. Keep the confirmed allowance of 20 managed-AI operations per calendar month. This is a validation price for at most the two confirmed cohorts of 100 AI paywall entrants each, not a commitment to the formal post-Beta price. Purchasers retain the lifetime Pro entitlement and disclosed monthly allowance if the price changes for later customers.

## Paid loop

For each cohort of 100 AI paywall entrants:

- At least 8 must purchase Pro.
- At least 5 must complete a paid AI output within seven days.
- Both conditions must be met to continue without changing the product hypothesis.

If the first cohort misses either threshold, make one targeted adjustment at the observed funnel break. If the second cohort of 100 AI paywall entrants still misses either threshold, stop investing in the AI family-trip printable-checklist position and return to research or repositioning. Stopping this position does not mean removing or discontinuing the legacy checklist capabilities.

## Paper use

Ask paid AI output completers once how the resulting checklist was ultimately used. A print success requires the user's confirmation that paper was produced and used during final packing.

For the first 20 paid AI output completers:

- 8 or more print successes: retain a paper-first position.
- 4 to 7 print successes: retain printing but reposition around PDF first and paper as an option.
- 0 to 3 print successes: reject paper as the core differentiator and revisit the product position.

No response is unknown and must not be counted as a print success.

## Managed-AI cost

For each purchase cohort, measure the managed-AI cost ratio over the first 30 days after purchase. Include all variable model, online-search, tool, and internal-retry costs, even when internal calls do not consume an additional user-visible AI operation.

- 5% or less: continue the one-time-purchase model.
- More than 5% and up to 15%: adjust the formal price, provider or model, prompt size, or online fact-checking strategy.
- More than 15%: do not adopt the $2.99 price with 20 monthly managed-AI operations as the formal commercial model, even if paid conversion succeeds.

Continue monitoring purchase cohorts after Beta because the first 30 days do not prove that a lifetime managed-AI entitlement is sustainable.
