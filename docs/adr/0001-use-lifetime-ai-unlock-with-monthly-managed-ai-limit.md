# Use a lifetime AI unlock with a monthly managed-AI limit

PrintableCheckList will use a one-time, restorable Pro purchase that permanently unlocks its AI functions. A user must purchase Pro before initiating the first real AI operation; the first version will not include free managed-AI operations or a complete free AI trial. Each Pro purchaser may initiate up to 20 managed-AI operations per calendar month. The limit resets each month, is not a stored or purchased credit balance, and unused capacity does not roll over. One AI operation is a user-initiated generation, regeneration, supplementation, or online fact check; internal provider calls, tool calls, and retries do not count separately. BYOK use is not subject to the managed-AI limit.

Manual checklist creation and editing, PDF export, and printing remain free. The first version will not offer trip credits, point packs, an auto-renewing subscription, or template packs as its primary revenue source. This keeps the purchase promise simple for an episodic family-travel use case while bounding the continuing cost of managed AI. It also creates a lasting obligation to restore the Pro entitlement and provide the disclosed monthly managed-AI allowance to purchasers.
